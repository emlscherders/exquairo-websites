About the assessment

What is the Infant Motor Profile?

The Infant Motor Profile is a video-based assessment designed to evaluate motor behaviour in infants aged approximately 3 to 18 months.

It considers not only which motor skills an infant has achieved, but also the quality of the movements used to explore and interact with the environment.

By observing an infant in a range of positions and situations, the IMP captures both motor performance and qualitative aspects of motor behaviour.

The framework

Five complementary domains

The IMP combines one domain describing motor milestone performance with four domains that focus on qualitative aspects of motor behaviour.

01

Variation

The variety of motor behaviour and movement strategies an infant demonstrates.

02

Adaptability

The ability to select and adapt motor strategies to changing situations and goals.

03

Symmetry

The degree of symmetry in the infant's movements and posture.

04

Fluency

The smoothness and fluency of movements.

05

Performance

The motor milestones and skills achieved by the infant.

A broader picture

Together, the five domains provide a multidimensional profile of infant motor behaviour.

From observation to understanding

Clinical and research applications

The IMP can be used to describe infant motor behaviour in clinical follow-up and research. Repeated assessments may help researchers and clinicians examine developmental trajectories and changes over time.

Research has explored the IMP's reliability, validity, associations with neurological condition and its relationship with later developmental outcomes.

Who may be interested?

  • Paediatric physiotherapists
  • Paediatricians and child neurologists
  • Neonatal and follow-up teams
  • Developmental researchers
  • Professionals working with infants at risk of developmental disorders

The IMP is an assessment instrument and should be interpreted by appropriately trained professionals within the broader clinical context.
